## Account security checklist
- Register with the dedicated brand email controlled by the owner.
- Use a unique password and enable two-step verification.
- Add recovery options controlled by the owner.
- Never send passwords or one-time codes to an assistant or third party.
- Do not connect personal social profiles as the publishing identity.
- If a platform requires a real administrator profile or identity verification, complete it directly and comply with the platform's rules.
- Grant a scheduler only the minimum permissions required; revoke access when no longer needed.
