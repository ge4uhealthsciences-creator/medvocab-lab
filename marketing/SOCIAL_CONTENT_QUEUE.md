## Next experiments to improve views

1. **Hook A/B:** Compare a direct question (“Can you tell X from Y in 5 seconds?”) with a misconception hook (“Most students mix up X and Y”). Keep the rest of the video as similar as possible.
2. **Length:** Compare a 15–20 second cut with a 25–30 second cut for comparable educational topics; use average watch time and completion/full-video-watched rate where available.
3. **Opening visual:** Test a large question/term on frame one against a speaker-led opening. Put subtitles on screen immediately and remove introductory logos or slow lead-ins.
4. **Pacing:** Use one idea per shot, change the visual every 2–3 seconds and reveal the answer near the end without delaying it artificially.
5. **Topic mix:** Keep the next cycle diversified across Psychology, Social Work, Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Pharmaceutical Technology; avoid Dentistry-heavy posts in the immediate sequence.
