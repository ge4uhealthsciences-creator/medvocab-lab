Privacy Policy

GE4u Health Sciences · Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Tiếng Việt bên dưới

GE4u Health Sciences ("GE4u", "we") provides English-learning materials for health-sciences students at ge4u.trungminhle.com, on our social media channels and in the GE4u English Ward game. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

Information we collect

  • Order requests. When you request a learning pack, we receive the details you type in the form (your name and a contact method) together with the product you chose. We use them only to contact you about that order.
  • Game accounts. If you create an account for GE4u English Ward, Google Firebase Authentication stores your email address (or your Google sign-in). We store your game progress, daily usage and Premium status so the game works across devices.
  • Basic usage data. Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages, Google Firebase) may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability.

Social media publishing tools

GE4u uses the official APIs of TikTok, Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) and Google (YouTube) only to publish GE4u's own educational videos and posts to GE4u's own accounts, and to read the performance of those posts. We do not use these APIs to access, collect or store information about other users, and we do not sell or share any data obtained through them.

How we use and share information

We use information only to provide our learning materials, process orders, run the game and improve our content. We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers that host our services (GitHub, Google Firebase, Google Sheets) or when required by law.

Retention and your choices

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing ge4u.health.sciences@gmail.com. Game players can also sign out and ask us to delete their account.

Children

Our services are intended for university students and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact
